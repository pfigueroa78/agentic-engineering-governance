# Standards Position

EGASE v0.1 is experimental and pre-standardization.

The project may align conceptually with established work in software quality, AI risk management, AI management systems, secure software development, architecture decision traceability, observability, and operational reliability.

However, this repository does **not** claim formal conformance, certification, or compliance with ISO, IEEE, NIST, or any other accredited standard.

Future crosswalks must distinguish explicitly between:

- conceptual alignment;
- practical compatibility;
- formal conformance.

Formal conformance must not be claimed unless all applicable normative requirements and the required assessment basis are demonstrated.
