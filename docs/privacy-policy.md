# Public Case-Study Privacy Policy

This repository publishes methodological evidence, not client intelligence.

Unless explicit publication authorization exists, public material must not contain or enable reasonable inference of:

- client or organization identity;
- proprietary product or project names;
- country, city, region, or other geographic clues;
- distinctive regulated or industry sector clues;
- private repository, branch, issue, pull-request, deployment, or environment identifiers;
- proprietary business-domain entity names;
- customer-specific architecture names, vendors, or combinations that allow triangulation;
- confidential metrics, volumes, dates, or operational facts that materially narrow identification.

## Abstraction rule

Retain only information necessary to understand, reproduce, challenge, or falsify the engineering conclusion.

Prefer neutral nouns such as:

- enterprise platform;
- domain entity;
- external system;
- authorized operator;
- material policy;
- human journey;
- source/runtime evidence.

## Review checklist

Before publishing a case study, ask:

1. Could a knowledgeable third party identify the client from the combination of clues?
2. Are any project names, repository identifiers, or domain-specific nouns unnecessary to the methodological result?
3. Are geography, scale, dates, or vendor combinations more specific than needed?
4. Can the same engineering lesson be expressed with a neutral abstraction?

If identification remains reasonably possible without adding methodological value, generalize further or do not publish the detail.
