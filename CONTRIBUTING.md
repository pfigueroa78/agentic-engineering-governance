# Contributing

Contributions are welcome, especially independent implementations, counterexamples, failed experiments, and evidence that challenges the method.

## Contribution principles

- Keep the method tool- and model-neutral.
- Prefer evidence over ceremony.
- Do not add a mandatory artifact without a concrete failure mode or decision it improves.
- Separate hypotheses from demonstrated claims.
- Avoid overstating standards alignment or conformance.
- Preserve privacy and confidentiality.

## Public case studies

Do not submit client names, organization names, proprietary product names, private repository references, geographic clues, distinctive sector terminology, internal identifiers, or business-domain entities that could reasonably identify a confidential project unless explicit publication authorization is documented.

Prefer neutral abstractions such as `enterprise platform`, `external system`, `domain entity`, `authorized operator`, and `material policy` when the specific business noun is not necessary to understand the engineering lesson.

## Suggested contribution types

- specification clarification;
- QSEM/EAR schema improvement;
- verifier/tooling implementation;
- independent case study;
- standards crosswalk research;
- empirical measurements;
- documented failure mode.
